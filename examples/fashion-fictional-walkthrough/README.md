# Fictional Walkthrough: Transitional Utility Jacket

English | [中文](README.zh.md)

This is a **fully fictional, non-importable, non-client narrative sample**. It helps readers understand how Fashion Harness objects connect a Brief to research, analysis, three design directions, human review, delivery, and evaluation.

It does not prove a real market trend, designer satisfaction, sales, commercial effect, or final browser acceptance. URLs use the reserved `example.com` domain, and every person, project, comment, and value is illustrative.

## Scenario

A fictional team explores a lightweight women’s commuting jacket for the United States in Fall 2026. The Brief emphasizes layering, soft utility, and three clearly differentiated directions.

The narrative flow is:

1. create a `ResearchTask` and register two fictional `Source` objects;
2. form human-reviewable `Evidence` from fictional `Sample` objects;
3. run one `AnalysisRun` to produce a `TrendCluster` and a `DesignOpportunity`;
4. select the exact opportunity revision and create exactly three `DesignConcept` objects;
5. a fictional reviewer requests a change to the second direction;
6. the change means the earlier review no longer represents the latest content, so a new approval is required before `DesignDeliverable`;
7. mark `OutcomeAvailability` as `not_yet_observed` and record no sales or market metric;
8. Evaluation records rule results and an improvement candidate without automatically modifying domain objects.

## Three directions

- **Cropped Transit**: cropped, structured, and focused on urban commuting;
- **Soft Field Layer**: softer, layerable, and focused on everyday adaptability;
- **Modular Longline**: longer, modular, and focused on changing weather.
